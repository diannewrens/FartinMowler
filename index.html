About the persona

Fartin Mowler is a parody software architecture commentator for the AI era. The bit: if every paradigm shift needs a sober thought leader explaining its patterns, AI deserves one who admits the room smells weird.

This is satire, not impersonation. Any resemblance to respected software authors with better names and fewer digestive metaphors is part of the joke.
